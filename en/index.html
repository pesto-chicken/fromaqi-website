Brand Story
Nothing beats a home-cooked meal, so we make every last sauce ourselves.

FROMAQI started in front of Korea University in Anam-dong and has made sandwiches the same way for ten years. Every sauce is a house recipe developed together by our international staff.

We bake bread daily and make our own ricotta and pastrami.
